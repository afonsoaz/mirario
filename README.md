# Restaurante Mira Rio

Build a website for a restaurant. It is called "Restaurante Mira Rio", in Santo TIrso, Portugal.

It's design language should be familiar, traditional, but also airy and alluding to the river and the countryside. The soul of the restaurant is "D. Fernanda", who has run the restaurant for 30+ years, acting also as main cook. She feeds her sons and grandsons who go there still almost everyday. People know the food is quality because she trusts it to her own family.

Here is an old website of the restaurant (now not maintained) which has some photos you may use / inspire yourself in: https://mira-rio.eatbu.com/?lang=pt. Attached also a photo of D. Fernanda.
